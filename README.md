## Traditional Middle Eastern Dessert Restaurant & Online Ordering

Welcome to Malabi Palace, where authentic flavors meet modern convenience. We specialize in traditional malabi and other Middle Eastern desserts, offering both dine-in experiences and seamless online ordering for delivery.

## About Malabi Palace

Malabi Palace brings you the finest traditional malabi - a creamy, delicate dessert made with milk pudding, topped with rose syrup, crushed pistachios, and coconut flakes. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, ensuring every spoonful delivers authentic Middle Eastern flavors.

### Our Specialties
- **Classic Malabi** - Traditional rose-flavored milk pudding with pistachios
- **Chocolate Malabi** - Rich cocoa variation with chocolate shavings
- **Pistachio Malabi** - Enhanced with ground pistachios throughout
- **Seasonal Flavors** - Orange blossom, cardamom, and more
- **Traditional Accompaniments** - Knafeh, baklava, and Turkish delights
